How to play

Setup. Four 4×4 boards sit in a square, two dark and two light. Each of your two nearer boards ("home" boards) is one dark, one light. Every board starts with 4 of your stones on the edge nearest you and 4 of your opponent's on the far edge.

Each turn has two moves. First a passive move: slide one of your stones 1 or 2 spaces, any direction, on one of your own home boards. It can't jump another stone and can't land on one.

Then an aggressive move, on a board of the other colour, using the exact same direction and distance. This stone may push a single enemy stone ahead of it — off the edge, and it's captured — but it can never push through two stones, or push your own.

If no aggressive move matches your intended passive move, that passive move isn't legal — choose another.

Win by clearing every one of an opponent's stones from any single board.
